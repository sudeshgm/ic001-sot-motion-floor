- **KITTI yaw convention (resolved before any gap was computed).** The harness converts camera boxes to velodyne by rotating the full orientation with the rotation block of `inv(Tr_velo_cam)` and taking the yaw of the rotated length axis. ic001 GT (and Open3DSOT) use `yaw = −(rotation_y + π/2)`. Because `Tr_velo_cam` is not an exact axis permutation, the two differ by up to 7.97e-3 rad (0.46°) at frame 0; centres match to 1.4e-14 m. This is the same issue already recorded for MBPTrack, where the GT-convention yaw was derived from the raw camera quaternion. For TrackAny3D no raw camera box was dumped, so the camera `rotation_y` is recovered by inverting the harness formula in closed form from the dumped yaw and the scene calibration only (`ic001/kitti_yaw.py`; no GT, no scores). Checks: (a) applied to the MBPTrack harness CSV, the inverse reproduces the MBPTrack raw-quaternion GT-convention yaw to 9.8e-15 rad over all 6,424 KITTI Car OPE frames; (b) TrackAny3D frame-0 yaw error vs ic001 GT drops from 7.97e-3 to 4.4e-16 rad. KITTI Car OPE scored with the ic001 scorer against the ic001 GT: GT-convention yaw **73.40 / 85.21** vs the tracker's own **73.42 / 85.24** (ΔS = −0.024); as-dumped yaw 73.33 (ΔS = −0.093). The GT-convention yaw is used for all KITTI TrackAny3D scores. It is the closer match on every KITTI class and protocol (as-dumped ΔS up to −0.24 on Cyclist OPE vs ≤ 0.04 corrected). The residual Precision offset (≈ −0.03, yaw-independent) is the known IoU / distance implementation difference also seen for the other families. nuScenes boxes are already in the global frame (frame-0 error 0.0); no correction is applied there.
