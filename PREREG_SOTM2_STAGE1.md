# Pre-registration: SOT-M2 Stage 1 (small fine-tune pilot)

Frozen before any Stage 1 training or evaluation result exists. Follow-up to IC001 (`RESULTS.md`, "Final decision
(with TrackAny3D)": nuScenes Car, the pts<10 bin decides and holds 59% of frames) and to the SOT-M2 inference-only
kill test (`PLAN_SOTM2_TRAINING.md`, "Evidence so far"). Plan: `PLAN_SOTM2_TRAINING.md`. Job spec:
`gpu_jobs/sotm2_stage1.md`. Code: `gpu_jobs/sotm2/` (tests: `tests/test_sotm2_stage1.py`).

## 1. Question and hypothesis

Inference-only, an oracle-aligned 10-sweep target-frame buffer adds +11.14 [+8.20, +14.24] Success on pts<10 for
M2-Track, but a self-aligned buffer used with the unchanged checkpoint is −4.50 versus ego-frame accumulation.
The open question is whether **training** recovers that headroom.

**H1:** With an equal fine-tuning budget, arm D (self-aligned target-frame buffer, keyframe-only loss) beats the
better of the two binding baselines, C (ego-frame 10-sweep accumulation, retrained) and I (sweep-rate training on
interpolated boxes), on nuScenes Car frames with < 10 LiDAR points or on moving frames.
**H0:** D is no better than max(C, I) in either cell.

## 2. Tracker, checkpoint, arms

- Tracker: M2-Track only (Open3DSOT `f08451d`), from the public nuScenes Car checkpoint `mmtrack_nuscenes_car.ckpt`
  (sha256 `3b19f52cc20c07f3f30cc0bf903c50f3bbd7039315785643b21a8a35332f0270`; trained on `train_track`).
  Model code, losses and sample construction are Open3DSOT's own (`motion_processing`, `compute_loss`); only the
  point clouds fed to them change.
- Fine-tuned arms (identical budget, §5):
  - **C** — each frame = its sweep + up to 9 earlier sweeps cut to the search region (box·1.25 + 2 m) around the
    causal rollout box at that sweep, kept in the world frame (ego-motion compensated). Binding baseline.
  - **I** — single-sweep pairs (j−1, j) at sweep rate (20 Hz), labels = devkit-interpolated GT boxes; tested
    causally at sweep rate from the frame-0 GT box. Binding baseline.
  - **D** — as C, but earlier-sweep points are moved rigidly from the rollout box at sweep j to the rollout box at
    the keyframe (self-aligned target-frame buffer); loss at keyframes only. The method.
  - **A_ft** — keyframe pairs, single sweep (fine-tune control). Reported only; trained after C, I, D if time remains.
- References (no training): **A** checkpoint at keyframe rate, **B** checkpoint at sweep rate (its causal boxes give
  the C/D buffers at test time, exactly as in the kill test). CV at sweep rate, freeze-if-static and the IC001 motion
  floor may be added later on CPU as reported references; they never enter the rule.

## 3. Data

- **Training:** nuScenes `train_track` Car tracklets (vehicle.car; first box num_lidar_pts > 0; ≥ 2 keyframes)
  from 30–50 scenes. Rank = `numpy.random.default_rng(0).permutation` of the sorted 350 `train_track` names
  (`data_manifest/sotm2_stage1_train_track_rank.txt`, generated by `gpu_jobs/sotm2/select_scenes.py`). Source
  archive: `v1.0-trainval07_blobs.tgz` (streamed, never stored). Subset = the first **40** ranked scenes whose
  every needed LIDAR_TOP sweep is present; < 30 such scenes → stop and report (no silent shrink). The chosen list
  is written to `sotm2_stage1_train_scenes.txt` with the crops and copied into `data_manifest/` before training.
  Crops: axis-aligned box corners ± 5 m around GT (keyframes) / interpolated GT (sweeps), xyz, global frame.
- **Evaluation:** the kill-test set, unchanged: nuScenes v1.0-mini, 9 scenes outside `train_track`, 215 Car
  tracklets, 4,459 non-first keyframes, 44,693 sweeps; crops ± 10 m (Open3DSOT preload). Bins fixed from
  single-sweep keyframe GT before any run: `bins.csv` sha256 `fd274b41bd070df761656d6ea68da1594f921259cacf5e34b5476dbbb6c7243e`
  (moving = keyframe GT step ≥ 0.5 m: 1,116 frames; pts<10: 2,482; both: 587). No training scene is in this set.

## 4. Metric and statistics

- Open3DSOT Success (trapz over 21 IoU thresholds in [0, 1], 3D IoU) and Precision (21 distance thresholds in
  [0, 2] m), ×100, **OPE** from the frame-0 GT box, scored at keyframes. Cells use non-first keyframes.
- **Headline cell: nuScenes Car pts<10** (as in IC001). Co-primary cell (plan): **moving**.
- Paired tracklet bootstrap: one multinomial tracklet draw per resample shared by all arms, **10,000 resamples,
  seed 0**, 95 % percentile CI, SE = bootstrap SD. Contrast per cell: **Δ = D − max(C, I)**, max taken inside every
  resample (and on the point estimate). Code: `gpu_jobs/sotm2/decide.py`.

## 5. Training budget (equal for C, I, D, A_ft)

2,000 optimizer steps × batch 100 per arm; Adam (lr 1e-4 constant, betas 0.5/0.999, eps 1e-6, wd 0, Open3DSOT
optimizer settings), Open3DSOT augmentation on, 4 candidate offsets, seed 0. Keyframe arms (C, D, A_ft) draw the
identical (tracklet, keyframe, candidate) stream; I draws sweep pairs with the same seed. The model scored is the
step-2,000 checkpoint (no early stopping, no validation-based selection).

## 6. Gates (must pass, else no verdict)

- **G1:** checkpoint arms A, B on kill-test tracklets 12, 13 reproduce the box-CPU reference, max |ΔIoU| ≤ 1e-4.
- **G2:** full-set arm A overall Success (incl. first frames) = 53.95 ± 0.1 and arm B = 39.40 ± 0.1 (kill test).
- **G3:** C, I and D all reached 2,000 steps with finite loss; frames.csv has 4,674 rows per arm; b_sweep_boxes.csv
  44,693 rows; output checksums verify.

## 7. Stage 1 decision rule (applied once, at step 2,000)

- **GO:** in pts<10 **or** moving: Δ ≥ +2.0 Success, 95 % CI lower bound > 0, and Δ ≥ 2 × SE.
- **KILL:** in pts<10 **and** moving: Δ ≤ +0.5 with 95 % CI upper bound < +2.0.
- **INCONCLUSIVE:** anything else.
- A KILL is soft (small data, few epochs can understate a method that must learn alignment); it is reported as
  "no training benefit at this budget". Stage 2 (full study, `PLAN_SOTM2_TRAINING.md`) is considered only after GO.
- Reported, not decisive: Δ with each of C and I separately, D − A, C − A_ft, I − A_ft, A_ft − A, Precision for every
  contrast, all per-arm Success/Precision in overall / pts<10 / moving / moving∧pts<10.

## 8. Compute

Free Kaggle T4 only, $0. **Chunk 1 hard cap 6,900 s wall (1.92 T4-h)**, enforced by the driver
(`gpu_jobs/sotm2/driver.sh`); checkpoint after every 500-step block, so a further chunk resumes exactly. Expected:
chunk 1 completes Stage 1 (≈ 1.3–1.9 T4-h). Stage 1 never exceeds 3 chunks (≤ 6 T4-h); any chunk beyond the
first is recorded in the run log before it starts. The training-set rollout boxes (arm B protocol, checkpoint) are
computed on CPU before the GPU run (0 T4-h).

## 9. Reportable failures (reported in plain words, no verdict)

G1/G2/G3 failure; cap reached before C, I, D reach the budget and no further chunk is run; non-finite loss; fewer
than 30 complete training scenes; any checksum or row-count mismatch; any deviation from §2–§5 (listed in the run
log). A failure is never converted into a KILL.

## Open choices (defaults taken)

1. Training source: blob 07 (it contained no val keyframes in the IC001 keyframe stream, so its scenes are all
   train-split); n = 40 (plan: 30–50), minimum 30; rank seed 0.
2. Training crop ± 5 m (search region is box·1.25 + 2 m, jitter ≤ 0.3 m); evaluation crops stay ± 10 m.
3. Budget 2,000 steps × 100, lr 1e-4 constant (plan: "a few epochs"; ≈ 5 passes over keyframe pairs).
4. D's alignment boxes come from the frozen checkpoint's causal sweep-rate rollout (train and test), no gradient
   through the alignment (stop-gradient, truncation length 1). Recurrent alignment learned end-to-end is Stage 2.
5. C crops earlier sweeps around the same rollout boxes (train and test), so C and D differ only in alignment.
6. I keeps the checkpoint's time-channel constants (0 / 0.1) and samples sweep pairs uniformly.
7. Evaluation = mini 9 scenes only; no val slice (val sweeps are not on hand; only val keyframes are).
8. Stage 1 runs M2-Track only (plan); TrackAny3D / MBPTrack are Stage 2.
9. max(C, I) inside each resample (conservative); bootstrap seed 0 (kill test used 20261122).
10. A_ft is trained after C, I, D only if the cap allows; it never blocks the rule.
11. Final-step checkpoint, no early stopping.
